Trade Marketing Campaign

Cheers Birthday Campaign

Objective

Drive store traffic and revenue during the brand anniversary period through integrated in-store promotions and shopper engagement.

Target Audience

Existing convenience store shoppers and nearby foot traffic — with a focus on increasing basket size and repeat visits.

My Role

Campaign lead — planning, POSM development, cross-functional coordination, store communication, and performance monitoring.

Challenge

The birthday campaign required synchronized deployment across 200+ stores within a tight timeline. Key challenges included:

  • Coordinating POSM production and delivery to arrive simultaneously at all locations
  • Aligning promotional stock levels with Purchasing to avoid out-of-stock during peak demand
  • Ensuring consistent visual execution across stores with varying layouts and team sizes
  • Managing multiple vendor relationships under time pressure

Strategy & Approach

  • Designed a multi-mechanic promotion strategy: combo deals, loyalty rewards, and lucky draws to maximize engagement
  • Created a detailed project timeline with milestones for each cross-functional team
  • Developed store-specific communication packages with visual guides for consistent setup
  • Built a POSM tracking system to monitor production, delivery, and in-store placement

Execution

  • Briefed and managed design agency for all campaign visuals and POSM (standees, posters, shelf talkers, wobblers)
  • Coordinated with Purchasing for promotional inventory planning and supplier alignment
  • Conducted store briefing sessions and distributed detailed execution guides
  • Ran daily monitoring during campaign launch week to address in-store issues immediately
  • Managed stakeholder updates and weekly progress reports to Marketing leadership

Results

0+ Stores Activated
0%↑ Traffic Increase
100% POSM Coverage
On Time Full Delivery

💡 Key Learnings

  • Preparation wins: Thorough planning in the first two weeks eliminated 80% of potential rollout issues.
  • Communication is everything: Clear, visual store guidelines reduced execution errors dramatically.
  • Cross-functional sync: Weekly alignment meetings with all stakeholders were the single biggest success factor.
Seasonal Activation

Halloween Store Activation

Objective

Create a memorable in-store experience during Halloween season to attract younger shoppers and increase basket size through themed promotions.

Target Audience

Young urban shoppers (18-35) seeking seasonal experiences and impulse-purchase driven consumers.

My Role

Concept development, POSM coordination, store display planning, campaign monitoring, and post-campaign evaluation.

Challenge

  • Competing for shopper attention against multiple brand activations during the same period
  • Limited budget requiring creative, high-impact solutions over expensive installations
  • Short campaign window demanding rapid deployment and immediate shopper engagement

Strategy & Approach

  • Focused on immersive store transformation rather than traditional discounting — creating an experience-led campaign
  • Designed cost-effective, high-impact POSM that doubled as seasonal decoration (themed shelf talkers, hanging displays, window graphics)
  • Bundled complementary products into Halloween-themed combos to drive upselling
  • Created Instagram-worthy photo spots in-store to drive organic social media exposure

Results

0+ Stores Activated
0%↑ Basket Size
High Engagement
Under Budget

💡 Key Learnings

  • Experience over discount: Creative in-store environments drove stronger engagement than price reductions alone.
  • POSM efficiency: Dual-purpose materials (decoration + sales tool) delivered higher ROI on limited budgets.
  • Social amplification: Photo-worthy store setups generated organic reach that extended the campaign beyond physical stores.
Mega Campaign

Lunar New Year (Tết) Campaign

Objective

Maximize revenue during the highest-spending period of the year through a multi-phase campaign featuring gift sets, combo promotions, and festive store transformations.

Timeline

8-week campaign spanning pre-Tet, peak Tet, and post-Tet phases — the largest campaign of the year.

My Role

End-to-end campaign ownership — from proposal and budget planning to execution, stakeholder management, and final performance report.

Challenge

  • Tight timeline with compressed planning phase due to the scale of the campaign
  • Multiple SKU promotions requiring precise coordination with Purchasing and suppliers
  • Large-scale POSM production (15+ material types) across 200+ stores
  • High-stakes campaign with direct executive visibility on performance

Strategy & Approach

  • Broke the campaign into three distinct phases (Pre-Tet / Peak / Post-Tet) with tailored mechanics for each
  • Created tiered gift set bundles at different price points to capture a wide shopper base
  • Designed a comprehensive POSM suite — from large-format standees to shelf-level price tags — ensuring full store coverage
  • Implemented weekly cross-functional check-ins with Marketing, Purchasing, Finance, Store Ops, and Logistics

Results

0+ Stores Deployed
0%↑ Revenue vs Target
0+ POSM Types
Top Campaign of Year

💡 Key Learnings

  • Phased approach wins: Breaking a mega campaign into phases allowed better quality control and mid-campaign optimization.
  • Stakeholder rhythm: Weekly check-ins with all teams created a cadence that prevented bottlenecks and surprises.
  • Plan for scale: Detailed timelines with buffer periods are essential for campaigns with this level of complexity.
